About MCCG

Built for maritime security, rapid deployment, and operational superiority, MCCG serves as a highly coordinated Coast Guard force operating within the harbors and coastline of Highrock.

Port & Harbor Security

MCCG maintains operational control over critical port zones, harbor checkpoints, and coastal response sectors.
